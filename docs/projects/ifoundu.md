## What it is

A web app that lets people **buy/sell the missing pair** of their product —
earbuds, earrings, shoes, etc. An open-source platform that helps users around
the globe keep using what they have and reuse their product instead of letting
it lie around the house.

## Purpose

Reduce waste by matching people who lost half of a product with people who have
the matching half.
